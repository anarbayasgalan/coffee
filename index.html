Estella Legacy Coffee

Three Generations, One Purpose

Three Generations of Excellence

1912 - The Beginning

Andrés Gutiérrez Bucardo planted the first coffee seeds received from German growers in Jinotega, Nicaragua.

The Professionalization

His son Gonzalo transformed the family farm into a professional enterprise with technical precision.

The Estella Legacy

Estella led the farm through political unrest, ensuring its resilience and growth with unwavering strength. We are a one-of-a-kind coffee that honors the legacy of a woman coffee grower, is led by a woman, and offers traceability grounded in a living history.

Purpose & Mission

To produce exceptional coffee while preserving our environment and supporting our local community through sustainable practices.

Code of Conduct

Integrity, quality, and respect for nature guide every decision we make from seed to cup.
